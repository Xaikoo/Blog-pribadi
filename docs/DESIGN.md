# Blog Pribadi — Design Direction

## Reading this as
A personal editorial journal for projects, experiments, technology, and lived process.

## Visual language
Monochrome editorial: black, white, thin rules, strong type scale, restrained serif contrast, and generous whitespace.

## Liveliness dials
- ENERGY: 2/5 — quiet confidence; no decorative noise.
- RHYTHM: 3/5 — varied type scale and section pacing keep long pages alive.
- MOTION: 1/5 — no decorative motion; respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Layout architecture
- `/` — editorial landing page and latest writing.
- `/writing` — chronological writing archive.
- `/posts/[slug]` — long-form reading surface.
- `/about` — author/site context.

## Deliberate exclusions
No fake metrics, testimonials, logos, stock imagery, gradients, glassmorphism, generic feature-card grids, or invented articles. Empty states are explicit and honest until real content exists.
